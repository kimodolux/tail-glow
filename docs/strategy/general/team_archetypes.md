# Team Archetypes

## Hyper Offense (HO)
**Goal:** Overwhelm opponent before they can respond
**Composition:** Lead setter + 4-5 sweepers/breakers
**Playstyle:**
- Set hazards turn 1 with suicide lead
- Apply constant pressure
- Trade Pokemon favorably
- Don't switch unnecessarily
**Weakness:** Priority, faster scarfers, bulk

## Balance
**Goal:** Flexible responses to any threat
**Composition:** Mix of offense and defense, good role coverage
**Playstyle:**
- Identify opponent's team archetype early
- Preserve your checks to their threats
- Build chip damage over time
- Win the long game
**Weakness:** Very optimized HO, strong breakers

## Stall
**Goal:** PP stall and passive damage
**Composition:** Walls, Toxic users, recovery, hazard stack
**Playstyle:**
- Set hazards and keep them up
- Spread status (Toxic, burns)
- Cycle defensive Pokemon
- Win by opponent running out of PP or fainting to chip
**Weakness:** Taunt, setup sweepers, wallbreakers

## Weather Teams
**Goal:** Abuse weather-boosted speed/power
**Composition:** Weather setter + 3-4 abusers + coverage
**Playstyle:**
- Get weather up ASAP
- Apply pressure with boosted sweepers
- Have backup setter if weather war expected
**Weakness:** Opposing weather, bulky resists

## Trick Room
**Goal:** Reverse Speed tiers to sweep
**Composition:** TR setter + slow powerful attackers
**Playstyle:**
- Set Trick Room safely
- Sweep with slow powerhouses
- Can bluff non-TR team
**Weakness:** Taunt, fast Encore, stalling out TR turns
