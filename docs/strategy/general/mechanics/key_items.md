# Key Items

**Choice Items**
- Choice Band: 1.5x Attack, locked to one move
- Choice Specs: 1.5x Sp.Atk, locked to one move
- Choice Scarf: 1.5x Speed, locked to one move

**Offensive Items**
- Life Orb: 1.3x damage, 10% recoil per attack
- Expert Belt: 1.2x damage on super-effective hits
- Assault Vest: 1.5x Sp.Def, cannot use status moves

**Defensive Items**
- Leftovers: Restore 1/16 HP per turn
- Heavy-Duty Boots: Immune to entry hazards
- Rocky Helmet: Attacker takes 1/6 HP on contact

**Utility Items**
- Focus Sash: Survive one KO hit at 1 HP (full HP only)
- Eject Button: Switch out when hit
- Red Card: Force opponent to switch when hit
