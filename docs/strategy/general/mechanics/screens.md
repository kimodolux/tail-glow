# Screens

**Reflect**
- Duration: 5 turns (8 with Light Clay)
- Halves physical damage to team
- Ignored by critical hits
- Removed by: Brick Break, Psychic Fangs, Raging Bull

**Light Screen**
- Duration: 5 turns (8 with Light Clay)
- Halves special damage to team
- Ignored by critical hits
- Removed by: Brick Break, Psychic Fangs, Raging Bull

**Aurora Veil**
- Duration: 5 turns (8 with Light Clay)
- Halves both physical and special damage
- Requires Snow to be active
- Key users: Ninetales-Alola, Abomasnow
