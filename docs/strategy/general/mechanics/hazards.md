# Entry Hazards

**Stealth Rock**
- Deals damage on switch based on Rock weakness
- 50% to 4x weak (Charizard, Volcarona)
- 25% to 2x weak
- 12.5% neutral
- 6.25% to resists
- Priority: Most important hazard to set AND remove

**Spikes (3 layers)**
- Layer 1: 12.5% damage
- Layer 2: 16.7% damage
- Layer 3: 25% damage
- Ground-immune Pokemon unaffected
- Stack with Stealth Rock for heavy chip

**Toxic Spikes (2 layers)**
- Layer 1: Poisons on switch
- Layer 2: Badly poisons on switch
- Poison types absorb them
- Flying/Levitate immune

**Sticky Web**
- Lowers Speed by 1 stage on switch
- Ground-immune unaffected
- Devastating for offensive teams

**Hazard Removal**
- Rapid Spin: Removes your side's hazards
- Defog: Removes BOTH sides' hazards
- Court Change: Swaps hazards between sides
- Magic Bounce: Reflects hazards back
