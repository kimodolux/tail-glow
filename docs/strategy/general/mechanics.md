# Battle Mechanics

## Weather

**Sun**
- Set by: Sunny Day (move) or Drought (ability)
- Fire moves: 1.5x damage
- Water moves: 0.5x damage
- Solar Beam: No charge turn
- Chlorophyll: Doubles Speed
- Key users: Torkoal, Koraidon, Walking Wake
- Counter: Set opposing weather, use Rock/Ground types

**Rain**
- Set by: Rain Dance (move) or Drizzle (ability)
- Water moves: 1.5x damage
- Fire moves: 0.5x damage
- Thunder/Hurricane: 100% accuracy
- Swift Swim: Doubles Speed
- Key users: Pelipper, Kyogre, Politoed
- Counter: Set opposing weather, use Grass/Electric types

**Sand**
- Set by: Sandstorm (move) or Sand Stream (ability)
- Rock types: 1.5x Sp.Def
- Damages non-Rock/Ground/Steel each turn
- Sand Rush: Doubles Speed
- Key users: Tyranitar, Hippowdon, Gigalith
- Counter: Set opposing weather, use immune types

**Snow**
- Set by: Snowscape (move) or Snow Warning (ability)
- Ice types: 1.5x Defense
- Aurora Veil: Usable
- Slush Rush: Doubles Speed
- Key users: Abomasnow, Ninetales-Alola, Chien-Pao
- Counter: Set opposing weather, use Fire/Fighting types

## Entry Hazards

**Stealth Rock**
- Deals damage on switch based on Rock weakness
- 50% to 4x weak (Charizard, Volcarona)
- 25% to 2x weak
- 12.5% neutral
- 6.25% to resists
- Priority: Most important hazard to set AND remove

**Spikes (3 layers)**
- Layer 1: 12.5% damage
- Layer 2: 16.7% damage
- Layer 3: 25% damage
- Ground-immune Pokemon unaffected
- Stack with Stealth Rock for heavy chip

**Toxic Spikes (2 layers)**
- Layer 1: Poisons on switch
- Layer 2: Badly poisons on switch
- Poison types absorb them
- Flying/Levitate immune

**Sticky Web**
- Lowers Speed by 1 stage on switch
- Ground-immune unaffected
- Devastating for offensive teams

**Hazard Removal**
- Rapid Spin: Removes your side's hazards
- Defog: Removes BOTH sides' hazards
- Court Change: Swaps hazards between sides
- Magic Bounce: Reflects hazards back

## Terastallization

**When to Tera:**
- Gain STAB on coverage move for KO
- Remove a critical weakness
- Survive a hit you'd otherwise die to
- Late-game sweeper boosting damage

**When NOT to Tera:**
- Early game (save for crucial moment)
- When opponent still has checks to your Tera type
- Just to resist one hit if you'll still lose the mon
- When opponent can easily switch to a counter

**Tera Type Selection:**
- Offensive: Match strong coverage move (Tera Normal on Dragonite for Extreme Speed)
- Defensive: Cover key weakness (Tera Fairy on Gholdengo vs Dark types)
- Surprise: Unexpected type to bait switches

## Terrain

**Electric Terrain**
- Set by: Electric Surge (ability) or Electric Terrain (move)
- Electric moves: 1.3x damage (grounded)
- Prevents Sleep on grounded Pokemon
- Surge Surfer: Doubles Speed
- Key users: Tapu Koko, Pincurchin, Miraidon

**Grassy Terrain**
- Set by: Grassy Surge (ability) or Grassy Terrain (move)
- Grass moves: 1.3x damage (grounded)
- Heals grounded Pokemon 1/16 HP per turn
- Earthquake/Bulldoze/Magnitude: 0.5x damage
- Grassy Glide: Gains priority
- Key users: Tapu Bulu, Rillaboom

**Psychic Terrain**
- Set by: Psychic Surge (ability) or Psychic Terrain (move)
- Psychic moves: 1.3x damage (grounded)
- Blocks priority moves targeting grounded Pokemon
- Key users: Tapu Lele, Indeedee
- Counter: Use non-priority moves, target non-grounded Pokemon

**Misty Terrain**
- Set by: Misty Surge (ability) or Misty Terrain (move)
- Dragon moves: 0.5x damage (grounded)
- Prevents status conditions on grounded Pokemon
- Key users: Tapu Fini, Weezing-Galar

## Speed Control

**Trick Room**
- Duration: 5 turns (including setup turn)
- Inverts speed order within each priority bracket
- Slowest Pokemon move first
- Key users: Hatterene, Porygon2, Uxie
- Counter: Stall out turns, use your own Trick Room, Taunt

**Tailwind**
- Duration: 4 turns (including setup turn)
- Doubles team's Speed
- Cannot be removed by Defog or Brick Break
- Stacks with Speed boosts (not capped at +6)
- Key users: Tornadus, Whimsicott, Talonflame

**Paralysis**
- Reduces Speed by 75%
- 25% chance to be fully paralyzed (lose turn)
- Electric types immune, Ground types immune to Thunder Wave
- Limber ability prevents paralysis

## Status Conditions

**Burn**
- Damage: 1/16 max HP per turn
- Halves physical attack damage
- Fire types immune
- Guts: Ignores attack reduction, 1.5x Attack
- Facade: Doubles to 140 BP when burned
- Key moves: Will-O-Wisp, Scald (30% chance)

**Poison**
- Regular: 1/8 max HP per turn
- Toxic (bad poison): Starts at 1/16, increases by 1/16 each turn
- Poison/Steel types immune
- Poison Heal: Restores 1/8 HP instead
- Key moves: Toxic, Poison Jab, Sludge Bomb

**Sleep**
- Duration: 1-3 turns (random)
- Rest: Always 2 turns
- Can only use Sleep Talk or Snore while asleep
- Insomnia/Vital Spirit: Immune
- Electric Terrain: Prevents sleep (grounded)

**Freeze**
- 20% chance to thaw each turn
- Fire moves thaw user: Flame Wheel, Flare Blitz, Sacred Fire, Scald
- Being hit by Fire move thaws target
- Ice types immune (except Tri Attack)
- Rarest status (no guaranteed move)

## Screens

**Reflect**
- Duration: 5 turns (8 with Light Clay)
- Halves physical damage to team
- Ignored by critical hits
- Removed by: Brick Break, Psychic Fangs, Raging Bull

**Light Screen**
- Duration: 5 turns (8 with Light Clay)
- Halves special damage to team
- Ignored by critical hits
- Removed by: Brick Break, Psychic Fangs, Raging Bull

**Aurora Veil**
- Duration: 5 turns (8 with Light Clay)
- Halves both physical and special damage
- Requires Snow to be active
- Key users: Ninetales-Alola, Abomasnow

## Stat Stages

**Stage Multipliers**
- +1: 1.5x | -1: 0.67x
- +2: 2.0x | -2: 0.5x
- +3: 2.5x | -3: 0.4x
- +4: 3.0x | -4: 0.33x
- +5: 3.5x | -5: 0.29x
- +6: 4.0x | -6: 0.25x

**Reset Conditions**
- Switching out
- Haze (resets all Pokemon)
- Clear Smog (resets target only)

**Critical Hits**
- Ignore attacker's negative stat stages
- Ignore defender's positive stat stages
- 1.5x damage multiplier

**Key Abilities**
- Intimidate: -1 Attack on switch-in
- Competitive: +2 Sp.Atk when stats lowered
- Defiant: +2 Attack when stats lowered
- Clear Body/White Smoke: Prevents stat drops

## Priority

**Priority Brackets (high to low)**
- +5: Helping Hand
- +4: Protect, Detect, Endure
- +3: Fake Out, Quick Guard
- +2: Extreme Speed, First Impression
- +1: Aqua Jet, Bullet Punch, Ice Shard, Mach Punch, Quick Attack, Shadow Sneak, Sucker Punch, Grassy Glide (in Grassy Terrain)
- 0: Most moves
- -1: Vital Throw
- -3: Focus Punch
- -5: Counter, Mirror Coat
- -6: Roar, Whirlwind, Dragon Tail, Circle Throw
- -7: Trick Room

**Priority Abilities**
- Prankster: +1 priority to status moves (fails vs Dark types)
- Gale Wings: +1 priority to Flying moves (at full HP only)
- Triage: +1 priority to healing moves

## Substitute

**Mechanics**
- Costs 25% max HP to create
- Blocks most moves and status
- Breaks when it takes damage exceeding remaining HP
- Does NOT block: sound moves, Infiltrator ability

**Strategic Use**
- Block status moves (Will-O-Wisp, Thunder Wave)
- Scout opponent's move choice
- Protect setup sweepers while boosting
- Key users: Setup sweepers, Baton Pass chains

## Key Items

**Choice Items**
- Choice Band: 1.5x Attack, locked to one move
- Choice Specs: 1.5x Sp.Atk, locked to one move
- Choice Scarf: 1.5x Speed, locked to one move

**Offensive Items**
- Life Orb: 1.3x damage, 10% recoil per attack
- Expert Belt: 1.2x damage on super-effective hits
- Assault Vest: 1.5x Sp.Def, cannot use status moves

**Defensive Items**
- Leftovers: Restore 1/16 HP per turn
- Heavy-Duty Boots: Immune to entry hazards
- Rocky Helmet: Attacker takes 1/6 HP on contact

**Utility Items**
- Focus Sash: Survive one KO hit at 1 HP (full HP only)
- Eject Button: Switch out when hit
- Red Card: Force opponent to switch when hit
