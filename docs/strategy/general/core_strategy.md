# Core Pokemon Battle Strategy

This document contains fundamental competitive Pokemon strategy concepts. Use these principles to guide decision-making throughout the battle.

---

## 1. Strategic Concepts

### Momentum
**Definition:** Having the opponent react to YOU, not vice versa

**Gaining Momentum:**
- Force switches with super-effective coverage
- Use U-turn/Volt Switch/Flip Turn to pivot
- Set up on predicted switches
- Double switch to bring in a check

**Losing Momentum:**
- Switching to walls repeatedly
- Predicting wrong and eating a hit
- Letting opponent set up for free

**Key Insight:** The player with momentum controls the pace. If you're always reacting, you're behind.

### Pivoting
**Definition:** Using switching moves to maintain momentum while scouting

**Pivot Moves:**
- U-turn (Physical, Bug)
- Volt Switch (Special, Electric)
- Flip Turn (Physical, Water)
- Teleport (Always goes last - safe pivot)
- Parting Shot (Lowers Atk/SpA before switching)
- Baton Pass (Passes stat changes)

**Pivot Playstyle:**
- Use pivots to scout opponent's switch-in
- Bring in the correct counter after seeing their play
- Maintain offensive pressure while staying safe
- Stack hazards + pivot = heavy chip damage

### Prediction
**Definition:** Anticipating opponent's move to gain advantage

**When to Predict:**
- You're behind and need to catch up
- Obvious switch situation (your sweeper vs their check)
- Endgame where one correct read wins

**When NOT to Predict:**
- You're ahead - play safe
- Risk is losing a crucial Pokemon
- Opponent is unpredictable/playing randomly

**Prediction Framework:**
1. What's their best play? (Usually safe option)
2. Do they know I know their best play?
3. What happens if I'm wrong vs right?
4. Is the risk worth the reward?

### Win Conditions
**Definition:** Your path to victory in this specific game

**Identifying Your Win Con:**
- Which of your Pokemon can sweep their remaining team?
- What needs to happen first? (Remove check, chip damage, setup)
- Is your win con still healthy?

**Protecting Your Win Con:**
- Don't let it take unnecessary chip
- Keep it in reserve until the path is clear
- Know what their counterplay is

**Denying Their Win Con:**
- Identify their sweeper/threat
- Keep your check healthy
- Chip their win con when possible

### Chip Damage
**Definition:** Small amounts of damage that accumulate to enable KOs

**Sources of Chip:**
- Entry hazards (Rocks + Spikes = 37.5% per switch)
- Weather damage (Sand, Hail)
- Status (Burn, Poison, Toxic)
- Recoil moves
- Life Orb (10% per attack)
- Rocky Helmet (16% on contact)

**Using Chip:**
- Chip their checks into KO range for your sweeper
- Force multiple switches to rack up hazard damage
- Toxic stall bulky Pokemon you can't break

## 2. Role Interactions

### Sweeper vs Wall
**Sweeper Goal:** Break through or set up
- Use coverage moves to hit wall
- Boost if wall can't threaten you
- Tera to change matchup

**Wall Goal:** Stop the sweep
- Use status to cripple (Toxic, Will-o-Wisp)
- Phaze out boosts (Roar, Dragon Tail, Whirlwind)
- Recover/protect to stall

### Setup Sweeper vs Revenge Killer
**Setup Sweeper Goal:** Get enough boosts to outspeed and OHKO
- Set up on passive Pokemon
- Tera to survive priority
- Know your Speed tier after boosts

**Revenge Killer Goal:** Stop the sweep before it starts
- Scarfed Pokemon to outspeed
- Priority moves (Extreme Speed, Ice Shard, Sucker Punch)
- Keep healthy throughout game

### Pivot vs Breaker
**Pivot Goal:** Maintain momentum and scout
- Switch out before taking big hit
- Bring in correct answer to their play
- Slowly wear them down

**Breaker Goal:** Punish switches with massive damage
- Use coverage to hit switch-ins
- Predict pivots and nail the incoming mon
- 2HKO most of the metagame

## 3. Decision Framework

### Turn-by-Turn Questions

1. **What can KO me?** (Calculate their damage)
2. **What can I KO?** (Calculate your damage)
3. **Am I faster?** (Speed determines who attacks first)
4. **What will they do?** (Switch? Attack? Setup?)
5. **What's the risk/reward?** (Staying in vs switching)

### When to Switch

**SWITCH when:**
- You'll be KO'd and have a better option
- You're doing nothing useful (they wall you)
- You need to preserve this Pokemon for later
- You can bring in something that threatens them

**STAY when:**
- You can KO them
- Switching gives them a free turn (setup)
- Nothing else on your team handles them better
- You're already dead (might as well attack)

### When to Setup

**SETUP when:**
- They can't KO you
- Their switch-in can't stop your sweep
- You need the boosts to break through
- It's late game and setup wins

**DON'T SETUP when:**
- They can KO you (attack instead)
- They have priority that KOs after boost
- They can phaze you out
- Early game when they have full team

### Endgame Principles

**When ahead:**
- Play safe, make simple trades
- Preserve your win condition
- Don't take unnecessary risks

**When behind:**
- Make aggressive predictions
- Look for any way back into the game
- Accept some risk is necessary

**When even:**
- Identify whose win condition is healthier
- Aim to trade favorably
- Every small advantage matters
